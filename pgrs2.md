## 🎯 Our Learning Strategy (Agreed)

Instead of spending many lessons on multiple mini-projects:

* ✅ Learn each concept with a **small runnable example**.
* ✅ Keep the pace fast to cover the complete React Native ecosystem.
* ✅ Build **one comprehensive production-grade capstone application** at the end that integrates everything naturally.

This approach gives you broad coverage first and then reinforces it by applying all the concepts together in a realistic application.
